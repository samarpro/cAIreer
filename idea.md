App idea

A2A Hire — a hiring platform where AI agents negotiate on behalf of candidates and employers, with humans only stepping in at the final decision.
Problem

Hiring is slow because it requires constant back-and-forth between two parties who are both approximating fit. Candidates undersell themselves in resumes. Employers write job descriptions that don't reflect what they actually need or where the role is heading. The result is a matching process that's shallow, reactive, and manual.
